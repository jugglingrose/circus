Spin Into Confidence: An Engaging School Assembly Experience

This high-energy, interactive hula hoop program inspires perseverance, confidence, creativity, and self-expression. Through dynamic performance blending hula hooping and circus arts, students experience engagaging moments that encourage resilience and a growth mindset.


Educational & Developmental Benefits

  • Helps student feel confident trying something new, even when it's challenging
  • Gets students excited about movement in a fun, positive way
  • Inspires creativity and expressing individuality
  • Demonstrates importance of persevering through challenges


What To Expect

  • High-energy hula hoop performances weaved throughout the program
  • Interactive moments with select student volunteers
  • Engaging storytelling around perserverance and growth
  • A fun, uplifting finale the leaves students inspired


Program Details

  • 45 minutes with a Q&A
  • Best suited for grades 1-5 (adaptable for younger audiences)
  • Recommended for smaller grade-level assemblies


Simple Setup

  • Cafeteria, gymnasium, or auditorium
  • Approximately 20X 20ft performance space

Hula Hoop Lab: Enrichment Workshop

Hula Hoop Lab is a fun, hands-on workshop where students learn beginner hula hoop tricks through guided instruction. Unlike the assembly, this session focuses on individual participation, practice, and skill building in a small-group setting.


Workshop Details

  • Ideal for Grades 1-5
  • 30 minute to 1 hour sessions
  • Up to 25 students per session
  • Multiple sessions can be booked in a day
  • Props are provided

Students Experience

  • Step-by-step instruction in beginner friendly hula hoop skills
  • Fun hula hoop games that encourage team building
  • Students leave with new skills they can confidently practice and build on
