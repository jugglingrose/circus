ABOUT

Corean is an Austin-based hula hoop performer and juggler known for her engaging stage presence and high-energy multi-hoop routines. With over a decade of experience, she blends playful choreography, colorful costuming, and dynamic performance. Sometimes spinning as many as 40 hula hoops at once!


From festivals and corporate events to schools assembly shows and summer library programs, Corean creates lively, joy-filled experiences that leave audiences of all ages smiling.
